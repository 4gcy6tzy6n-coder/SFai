# Research

When researching:

1. Search narrowly, then open the specific sources you cite.
2. Keep one bullet per claim and attach the exact `source://` ref next to it.
3. Never invent references: every citation must resolve through `context.read`.
4. If two sources disagree, report both and mark the conflict instead of
   averaging them away.

Finish with a short report that separates findings from citations.
